## Legal text

{{< abs 1 >}}
Union or Member State law to which the data controller or processor is subject may restrict by way of a legislative measure the scope of the obligations and rights provided for in Articles 12 to 22 and Article 34, as well as Article 5 in so far as its provisions correspond to the rights and obligations provided for in Articles 12 to 22, when such a restriction respects the essence of the fundamental rights and freedoms and is a necessary and proportionate measure in a democratic society to safeguard:
{{< lit a >}}national security;{{< /lit >}}
{{< lit b >}}defence;{{< /lit >}}
{{< lit c >}}public security;{{< /lit >}}
{{< lit d >}}the prevention, investigation, detection or prosecution of criminal offences or the execution of criminal penalties, including the safeguarding against and the prevention of threats to public security;{{< /lit >}}
{{< lit e >}}other important objectives of general public interest of the Union or of a Member State, in particular an important economic or financial interest of the Union or of a Member State, including monetary, budgetary and taxation a matters, public health and social security;{{< /lit >}}
{{< lit f >}}the protection of judicial independence and judicial proceedings;{{< /lit >}}
{{< lit g >}}the prevention, investigation, detection and prosecution of breaches of ethics for regulated professions;{{< /lit >}}
{{< lit h >}}a monitoring, inspection or regulatory function connected, even occasionally, to the exercise of official authority in the cases referred to in points (a) to (e) and (g);{{< /lit >}}
{{< lit i >}}the protection of the data subject or the rights and freedoms of others;{{< /lit >}}
{{< lit j >}}the enforcement of civil law claims.{{< /lit >}}
{{< /abs >}}

{{< abs 2 >}}
In particular, any legislative measure referred to in paragraph 1 shall contain specific provisions at least, where relevant, as to:
{{< lit a >}}the purposes of the processing or categories of processing;{{< /lit >}}
{{< lit b >}}the categories of personal data;{{< /lit >}}
{{< lit c >}}the scope of the restrictions introduced;{{< /lit >}}
{{< lit d >}}the safeguards to prevent abuse or unlawful access or transfer;{{< /lit >}}
{{< lit e >}}the specification of the controller or categories of controllers;{{< /lit >}}
{{< lit f >}}the storage periods and the applicable safeguards taking into account the nature, scope and purposes of the processing or categories of processing;{{< /lit >}}
{{< lit g >}}the risks to the rights and freedoms of data subjects; and{{< /lit >}}
{{< lit h >}}the right of data subjects to be informed about the restriction, unless that may be prejudicial to the purpose of the restriction.{{< /lit >}}
{{< /abs >}}
